# Conversation starters

Paste these into the four **Conversation starters** slots in the Custom GPT builder (Configure tab).
Keep them short — ChatGPT shows them as clickable chips.

1. `Build this week's Anki — here are my lectures and objectives`
2. `Map this lecture to AnKing leaf tags (I'll paste the slides)`
3. `Coverage-audit my deck against the lecture objectives`
4. `What's the undo for the cards I unsuspended this week?`

## Alternate starters (swap in as you like)

- `Which AnKing tags cover <lecture topic>?`
- `Propose a build plan for IM## — foundational lecture, go all-tier`
- `Trim off-lecture tangents from my <topic> deck`
- `Give me the new/day pacing to finish this week by Saturday`
